How to use S.P.A.R.K. AI

First launch
Open the app, then click the gear icon at the bottom of the sidebar to open Settings. Under "Brain," pick either Google Gemini (free) or OpenAI (paid), paste in your API key, optionally adjust the model name, and click Save.

The four sections
Switch between them using the icons at the top of the sidebar.

Doubts is where you ask any Physics, Chemistry, or Maths question in plain language — type in the box at the bottom and press Enter to send, or Shift+Enter for a new line. You can also attach or paste a screenshot of a problem.

Quiz lets you pick a Subject, Grade, and optionally a specific Topic or chapter, choose how many questions you want, and click Generate quiz. Answer each one, then submit to see your score along with an explanation for every question.

Planner is a study task list. Click "+ Add task" to add something manually, or just ask in Doubts — for example, "add three days of revision for thermodynamics" — and Spark updates the planner for you directly. Tap the checkbox on any task to mark it done.

Progress shows your quiz accuracy, both overall and broken down by subject, plus how many planner tasks you've completed. It updates automatically as you use the app.

Sidebar controls
Subject lets you choose Auto-detect, Physics, Chemistry, or Mathematics. Grade is Class 11 or Class 12, which keeps examples within that year's syllabus. Topic narrows things down to a specific chapter, and only becomes active once you've picked a specific subject rather than Auto-detect. Replies toggles between Quick (fast, to-the-point answers) and Deep (full derivations and explanations).

Profiles
Click your name or avatar at the bottom of the sidebar to add a profile. This lets Spark address you personally and keeps chat history, quiz history, and the planner separate if more than one person uses the app.

Getting a free API key
Go to aistudio.google.com and sign in with a Google account, click "Get API key," then "Create API key," and paste it into SPARK's Settings. If you hit a rate limit, switching to a Flash-Lite model gives you more free requests per day.

A few things worth knowing
Everything — chats, quizzes, the planner — saves locally in your browser, and nothing is sent anywhere except whichever AI provider you chose. If you open the app without running the local server, it still works, but Safari specifically won't save chat history that way (Chrome, Edge, and Firefox are fine either way). And if you install it as an app, it'll work offline and open in its own window instead of a browser tab.
